What is a CAS Statement?

A Consolidated Account Statement (CAS) is issued by NSDL or CDSL and contains details of all mutual fund investments linked to your PAN. It includes transactions, units held and NAV values across multiple fund houses.

The CAS document acts as a consolidated record of your investments but it is not always easy to interpret portfolio performance directly from the statement.

Challenges in Reading a CAS Statement

Most investors find it difficult to evaluate their portfolio using the raw CAS document because:

  • The document contains detailed transaction records
  • Portfolio allocation is not summarized
  • Returns such as XIRR are not calculated
  • Overall portfolio gain or loss is not clearly visible
  • Fund allocation must be calculated manually
