What is a CAS Statement?

A Consolidated Account Statement (CAS) is a document that lists all mutual fund investments linked to your PAN across different fund houses. It provides a single consolidated view of your holdings and transactions.

Key Information in CAS

Your CAS statement typically includes scheme names, investment transactions, current market value, folio numbers, and units held. Understanding these sections helps investors evaluate their portfolio performance.

What to Look For in Portfolio Analysis

  1. Check diversification across fund categories
  2. Identify overlapping fund holdings
  3. Review allocation across equity and debt funds
  4. Analyze portfolio concentration risk
  5. Evaluate overall portfolio return

Common Portfolio Issues Found in CAS

Many investors discover important insights when they analyze their CAS statement. One common issue is holding too many mutual funds that invest in similar stocks. This creates portfolio overlap and reduces diversification benefits.

Another common issue is unbalanced asset allocation. For example, some portfolios may be heavily concentrated in equity funds while others may hold too many similar large-cap schemes. Reviewing the CAS helps identify these issues early.

Regular portfolio review helps investors simplify their holdings and ensure their investments remain aligned with long-term financial goals.
