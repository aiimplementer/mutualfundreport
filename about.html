Why Portfolio Analysis Matters

Many investors hold multiple mutual funds believing their portfolio is well diversified. However several funds invest in the same underlying stocks creating hidden concentration risk.

Understanding the real structure of your portfolio helps improve diversification and reduce unnecessary exposure.
