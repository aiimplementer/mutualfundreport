Analyze Your Mutual Fund Portfolio in Seconds

Many investors hold multiple mutual funds thinking their portfolio is well diversified. However, in reality, several funds often invest in the same underlying stocks, creating hidden concentration and risk.

Understanding what is truly inside your mutual fund portfolio can be difficult without the right tools. This is where a portfolio analysis report becomes extremely useful.

A simple tool is now available that allows investors to generate a detailed Mutual Fund Portfolio Health Report in seconds.

Try the Free Portfolio Health Report Tool

You can generate your report using the tool available at:

👉 https://mutualfundreport.netlify.app/

The tool works instantly by analyzing your CAS (Consolidated Account Statement). The CAS is the official statement sent by CAMS and KFintech that contains all your mutual fund transactions across platforms.

How the Tool Works

  1. Download your CAS statement from CAMS or KFintech
  2. Upload the CAS PDF to the tool
  3. Generate your detailed portfolio health report instantly

Instead of manually analyzing each fund, the tool automatically reads your portfolio data and produces a structured report within seconds.

What Insights Does the Report Show?

The report highlights several important aspects of your portfolio that many investors usually miss.

  • Portfolio Concentration – Understand if too much money is invested in a few funds or stocks
  • Fund Overlap – Identify duplicate stock exposure across multiple mutual funds
  • Asset Allocation – See how your portfolio is distributed across categories
  • Risk Indicators – Detect potential risk signals within your portfolio
  • Portfolio Structure – Visual overview of your entire mutual fund holdings

Why Portfolio Analysis Matters

Many investors focus only on returns when evaluating mutual funds. But returns alone do not tell the complete story.

A strong portfolio should also maintain proper diversification, balanced risk exposure, and minimal overlap between funds.
