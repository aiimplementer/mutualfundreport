Mutual Fund Portfolio Overlap Explained

Many investors hold multiple mutual funds believing their portfolio is diversified. However several funds may hold the same stocks, creating hidden concentration risk.

Analyze Your Portfolio

What is Portfolio Overlap?

Portfolio overlap occurs when multiple mutual funds in your portfolio invest in the same underlying stocks. While each fund may appear different, the actual stock exposure can be very similar.

Why Portfolio Overlap Can Be Risky

If several funds hold the same top stocks, your portfolio may become concentrated without you realizing it. This reduces the benefits of diversification and increases exposure to specific companies or sectors.

How to Identify Fund Overlap

  1. Review holdings of each mutual fund
  2. Identify common stocks across funds
  3. Measure percentage overlap
  4. Evaluate if diversification is adequate
  5. Rebalance portfolio if needed
